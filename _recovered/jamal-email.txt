About Us

A trusted partner in leadership & transformation

 

Watson & Watson Associates, LLC is a strategic communications and consulting firm founded by Dr. Jamal Watson—a scholar, author, award-winning journalist and higher education strategist—with more than two decades of experience covering, advising, and speaking on the issues shaping American institutions today.

We work directly with college and university presidents, provosts, boards, nonprofit executives, and communications teams to sharpen institutional voice, strengthen strategy, and build the kind of leadership that endures beyond a single news cycle or budget year. Our approach blends the rigor of a journalist’s eye and the insight of a scholar of history and public policy.

Whether we're guiding an institution through a strategic communications plan, coaching an executive through a defining moment, or placing a speaker who can move a room, our work is grounded in one belief: institutions rise or fall on the strength—and the clarity—of their leadership.

 

 

Our Mission

To equip leaders and institutions with the strategy, storytelling, and voice they need to serve their communities with excellence, integrity, and lasting impact—spanning higher education, culture, and entertainment.

 

Our Vision

To create a nation of institutions led with clarity and courage, where colleges, universities, and mission-driven organizations are equipped to communicate their purpose, navigate change, and produce the next generation of transformative leaders.

Dr. Jamal Watson

Founder & Principal, Watson & Watson Associates, LLC

 

Dr. Jamal Watson is a professor, award-winning journalist, and author whose work sits at the crossroads of higher education, civil rights, and American political history. 

He is the former executive editor of Diverse: Issues In Higher Education, and is a professor of Strategic Communications and Public Relations at Trinity Washington University. His book, The Student Debt Crisis: America's Moral Urgency(Broadleaf Books, 2025), carries a foreword by Rev. Al Sharpton and makes the case for student debt as a defining civil rights issue of our time. The publication received a starred review from The Library Journal. 

Watson brings a rare combination of scholarly depth and newsroom instinct to every stage he steps onto. A recognized expert on higher education equity, and Black political and religious history, he speaks with the authority of someone who has spent a career asking institutions the hard questions and helping them answer well. 

Watson has received numerous awards, including the Ida B. Wells Journalism award from the Higher Education Leadership Foundation and the Frank W. Hale, Jr. Diversity Leadership Award from the National Association of Diversity Officers in Higher Education.

Dr. Melanie R. Hill (photo attached)

Author, Scholar and Violinist 

Dr. Melanie R. Hill is Named Term Chair Andrew W. Mellon Associate Professor of Global Racial Justice and Associate Professor of American Literature with tenure with specializations in African American Literature, Black Music Performance, and Womanist Literature/Thought/Theology in the Department of English at Rutgers University, Newark. Hill received her Ph.D. in English Literature with two graduate certificates in Africana Studies and Women, Gender, and Sexuality Studies from the University of Pennsylvania. Dr. Hill has been a reviewer for the Yale Journal of Music and Religion and published articles on the art of the sermon in African American literature, Black feminism/womanist theology, the literary and musicological histories of Black women preachers, and on the musicological works of blues and jazz vocalists Bessie Smith and Ella Fitzgerald under the James Baldwin Review, Religions Journal, and Oxford University Press, respectively. Dr. Hill has held positions as Associate Research Scholar and Visiting Assistant Professor of Religion and Literature at Yale University’s Institute of Sacred Music and Divinity School. Her newly released book, Colored Women Sittin’ on High:Womanist Sermonic Practice in Literature and Music (UNC Press), focuses on the ways in which Black women preachers in African American literature, music, and in the space of the pulpit counter social injustices through sermon and song. Colored Women Sittin’ on High has been officially endorsed and  praised by both Alice Walker, author of The Color Purple, and civil rights legend and theologian, Ruby Sales. In May 2026, Dr. Hill commemorated, through a violin performance, the 101st Birthday of revolutionary leader, Malcolm X and opened for the book signings of Dr. Ilyasah Shabazz, author, educator, social activist, and the daughter of Malcolm X. 

 

 

Featured Book

The Student Debt Crisis: America's Moral Urgency

 

The Student Debt Crisis: America's Moral Urgency (Broadleaf Books, 2025), with a foreword by Rev. Al Sharpton, makes the case that America's $1.7 trillion student debt crisis is one of the defining civil rights issues of our time. Drawing on extensive interviews with students, administrators, and policymakers, Watson traces the history and current reality of student debt in America and its disproportionate toll on Black and brown borrowers, particularly Black women— while making an urgent moral argument for reform.
